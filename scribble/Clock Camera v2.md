I built [[ClockCamera]] several years ago, based on Firebase and MUI. I learned a heap in the process, but wasn't sure I wanted to keep it running at the time (certainly the clock.camera domain quickly got too expensive.)

To get more familiar with [PocketBase](https://pocketbase.io/) and [Fly.io](https://fly.io/) I decide to rebuild it (and I swapped MUI out for [Chakra UI](https://chakra-ui.com/).) 

[The app is available here](https://cc.requisite.link/), and [the source is here](https://github.com/simonhildebrandt/cc2).
